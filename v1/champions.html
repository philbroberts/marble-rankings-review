SOS marbles is the average balance of opponents at the season snapshot, counting each included game once. F1 is the weighted harmonic mean of percentiles within each season (80% marbles, 20% SOS), on a 0–100 scale. Small italic numbers show each champion's rank among teams in its own season.

Pre-1988 seasons use Trono’s archive, with NCAA tournament dates approximated by round except for the title game. 1988–2001 use the version-pinned historical NCAA competition dataset. Historical score, date and venue caveats appear in the linked season ledgers. Colors and logos are display references; exact historical branding may differ.
