Marble methodology and sources

Expected margin = (this team’s marbles − opponent’s marbles) ÷ 10, plus 3 points at home, minus 3 away, or zero at a neutral site. Each point above expectation earns one marble from the opponent; each point below transfers one marble to the opponent. Balances can go negative. In the standard view each team starts at zero.

Only included Division I games affect these rankings. Game counts and records may differ from a team’s official overall schedule. Final scoring includes overtime. Some historical dates use opening-day ratings for every prediction, with transfers applied together; those closing balances are labeled in the ledger.
